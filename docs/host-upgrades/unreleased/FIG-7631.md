# FIG-7631: Programs run through the region ISA, with a new continuation format

| Old contract | New contract / host action | Commit and ticket | Missed-change diagnostic or symptom |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `lash::workflow::NativeSpecialization { immediate, resident, text_relation, text_append, .. }` has no resource certificate for its immediate entry. | It gains the public field `immediate_resources: bool`. A native that builds the struct with a full literal adds `immediate_resources: false`, or spreads `..NativeSpecialization::default()`. Set it to `true` only when every successful answer from `immediate` has exactly its immutable output as its resource obligation, with no scratch and no fresh mutable entity, in agreement with the native's `resource_contract`. No feature, configuration or policy opt-in is required. | This commit, FIG-7631 | Full struct literals fail to compile with E0063 (missing field `immediate_resources`). A native that leaves it `false` still runs, but the machine answers its calls; the region does not take its immediate entry in place. |
| Saved continuations use page format 13: frame roles are keyed by their source position. | Page format 14: each frame role is keyed by the digest of its full source discriminator, and a version carry rekeys saved roles. Reset affected stores or recreate retained checkpoints before deploying. No migration, old-shape reader or opt-in is provided. | This commit, FIG-7631 | The format-witness survey reports a changed witness, and checkpoints saved under format 13 are refused on reopen as unsupported bytes. |
